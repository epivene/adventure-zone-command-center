---
name: Personal booking-agent finances
description: Privacy and accounting boundary for the owner's booking-agent records and pay tracking.
---

Personal booking-agent documents, received income, expenses, earned pay and tax
planning belong only in owner Admin, separate from company/crew finances.
Never send these attachments through shared document classification or AI intake.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose "Only me in Admin" for personal booking-agent
tax documents and receipts; these are not general business records.

**How to apply:** Protect each endpoint and file download with owner authorization.
Do not expose personal finance data through Office, Boss, Manager, shared search,
public attachment URLs or shared document ingestion. Privacy is tied to the
existing owner login; it does not distinguish people sharing those credentials.

Earned pay and payments received are different accounting events. Add work
periods explicitly rather than guessing employment dates, and let received
payments create one linked income entry, not a second independently entered sum.

**Why:** The user needs both the balance still owed to them and tax planning.
Counting unpaid earnings as received cash or entering a payment twice would
distort both results.

**How to apply:** Preserve payment idempotency and the earned/received separation.
Tax estimates are cash-basis planning with explicit assumptions, year-specific
official rules and limitations, not guaranteed liabilities or automatic deductions.

Accountant handoffs must exclude tax-estimate assumptions by default and clearly
state that saved records and receipts are not proof of deductibility.

**Why:** The requested year-end handoff is for personal booking-agent evidence,
not disclosure of sensitive estimate inputs or a determination that expenses qualify.

**How to apply:** Keep future export formats and sharing flows within that scope;
do not silently add estimate settings to an accountant package.
